§ 00 · SYSTEM

A design language for numbers you can trust.

Signal is the interface system behind the Earnings & Catalyst Dashboard — a dense, dark, desktop-first analyst tool covering a personal watchlist. Every figure carries a freshness signal and a reachable source. The system is built to make provenance, staleness, and surprise legible at a glance.

01
Every number is sourced & fresh
No figure appears without a freshness dot and a reachable provenance on hover. The single load-bearing rule.
02
Type-aware by construction
Operating, developer, and ETF securities render different fields. Enforced in the component layer, not per page.
03
Honesty over completeness
Missing data reads as an explicit “—” with a stale flag — never a blank cell or a fabricated value.
04
Fail-soft, visibly
A dead source shows an inline chip; its siblings still render. Gaps turn a dot amber rather than hiding a field.

Color

A cool near-black canvas with layered navy surfaces. One electric blue carries brand and interaction; semantic hues are reserved strictly for meaning — beat/miss, guidance, and the freshness RAG scale. Color never carries meaning alone; it is always paired with a label or icon.

§ 03 · SPACING & LAYOUT

Spacing & Layout

A 4px base unit governs all spacing. Analyst views are intentionally dense — table rows and metric grids sit toward the tight end of the scale — while reading views (event detail, source viewer) breathe. Layout is a 12-column desktop grid at 1440px with 40px gutters.
